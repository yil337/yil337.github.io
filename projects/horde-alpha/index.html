Horde Alpha

An isometric stealth-and-infection strategy prototype. Players direct a zombie group, approach humans from behind and grow the horde through infection.

Group control

The player moves a rally point rather than a single character. Nearby zombies follow the anchor, creating a different control problem from direct movement in a conventional action game.

Stealth and escalation

Human behavior includes patrol, suspicion, investigation, combat and panic. Vision and sound influence reactions; a roar can draw guards away, while frightened civilians can raise alarms.

Infection and objectives

The prototype connects infection with different unit roles, group commands and mission evaluation. It remains an experimental local build, with no public release linked here.
