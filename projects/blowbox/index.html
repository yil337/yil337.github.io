Three ways to use breath

The candle responds to blowing, the dandelion disperses seeds, and the balloon converts sustained input into inflation and score. I built the toys as one iOS collection with visual, audio and haptic feedback.

Risk and reward

The balloon has a randomized burst threshold. Players can bank their current score or keep inflating for more, with local best scores and a share card. Sound, animation and haptics communicate growing tension.

Input iteration

Device testing showed that the initial spectral-flatness assumption did not reliably identify blowing. I revised detection around an adaptive RMS noise baseline and low-frequency energy, and handled microphone permissions and the audio lifecycle.

Project status

The implemented three-toy version was archived before App Store submission. The later breath-controlled paper-character concept is a separate proposal, not a shipped feature of BlowBox.
