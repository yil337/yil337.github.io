PartyBox

Eight party-game modes for a shared iPhone: writing, drawing, improv and voting, with private handoffs and a session-wide leaderboard.

Private turn-taking

I designed private handoff screens for a shared iPhone. Each handoff identifies the next player before revealing their prompt or input, keeping the previous player’s answer hidden.

Voting & scoring

I refined voting confirmation to reduce accidental choices, handled conflicting answers, and adjusted scoring for odd-sized voting groups. Input length, timers and participation limits vary with the activity. The purpose is to keep the rules readable and the session fair across different group sizes.

Session flow

Another-round flows and a session-wide leaderboard connect the eight modes. I adapted familiar party-game formats for one shared device, with bilingual prompts and instructions, then refined the handoffs, voting and scoring.

Social play

The modes use hidden information, player-written answers, voting and performance. Each activity has different requirements for timers, input limits and who participates. I adapted the interaction flow to those differences instead of applying one sequence to every game.
