Writing & direction

Director Murphy tries to get an AI to make melodramatic short films. Misunderstanding, correction and commentary form the comic loop. I developed the scripts and storyboards, then shaped the performance through shot selection, voice, sound and editing.

Character consistency

A consistent character and voice provide an anchor while the AI-generated scenes change. The work includes character references, production notes and a parameterized Remotion editing workflow. The illustrated portrait shown here is a character reference from the project.

Completed episodes

Two final episodes are complete, each approximately a minute and a half long. Episode 1 is playable on this page in its original Chinese-language format, with dialogue and on-screen captions.
