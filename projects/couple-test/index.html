Quiz, pairing & reports

The product connects a questionnaire, a personal profile and a paired report. Pairing links let two people contribute separately before seeing a comparison. I worked on the full-stack flow with Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase.

Scoring model

Weighted scoring organizes answers into a readable report. The value is in making similarities and differences easy to explore. This is an entertainment-oriented rule model, not a clinically validated psychological assessment.
