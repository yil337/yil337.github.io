The whole production process

At Bauhinia AI, I worked across the tools and content of an AI game-production platform. The team’s work connected creative briefs, editing and administrative interfaces, automated execution, validation, review and publishing.

My contributions

I contributed human approval checkpoints, visibility into running jobs, reusable game templates, versioned Workflow Packs, a local MCP server, and build and artifact validation. These components helped make production work inspectable and repeatable, while keeping design and release decisions visible to people.

Games and interactive content

Alongside platform work, I developed and iterated playable games and interactive character scenes, working with rules, animation, scene states and audiovisual feedback. I also worked on AI training using a collection of more than 1,000 HTML5 game source files.

What the work taught me

A generated build is the start of evaluation. Someone still needs to judge the rules, pacing and experience, revise the content, and decide whether it is ready to release. Building both content and production tools deepened my understanding of that relationship.
