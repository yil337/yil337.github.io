Escape the Horde

An asymmetric horror prototype for 2–3 players. Survivors try to escape a manor while an opponent commands and possesses a finite infected force.

My roleDesigner & developer

Asymmetric multiplayer

The survivors and the opposing player inhabit the same situation with different goals and different kinds of control. Survivors navigate danger and branching escape objectives. Their opponent manages a limited infected force, choosing where to apply pressure and when to take direct possession.

Stealth & counterplay

Survivor movement and noise feed into sound-based detection. Players need to balance advancing their escape objectives against revealing their location to the opposing side.

Networking & AI

I implemented branching escape quests, server-authoritative WebSocket multiplayer and AI-controlled survivors. The server maintains shared gameplay state so that players act within the same match.

Prototype scope

The browser prototype includes survivor and horde roles, branching escape objectives, infected deployment and possession. It is a playable prototype, rather than a released commercial game.
