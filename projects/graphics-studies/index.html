From mathematics to a visible result

Within the course framework, I implement sampling, coordinate transformations, textures and lighting, then connect ray intersections to shadows and reflections. This work gives me a practical foundation for understanding how a scene is rendered.

Tracing and acceleration

My Slang work includes triangle and bounding-box intersection, BVH traversal, interpolated normals, normal transforms and reflection paths. The exercises connect the mathematical model to both image behavior and the amount of work required to produce it.

Scenes and course scope

The gallery contains saved coursework outputs, including custom scene work. CSE167 is in progress in Fall 2026; these are implementations in a provided teaching framework, rather than a complete commercial renderer built from scratch.
