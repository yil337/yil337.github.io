Solo production

I handle gameplay and rules, art and audio production and integration, programming, engineering, and the content editor. I also prepare TestFlight builds, App Store Connect listings, privacy pages, store artwork, trailers, advertising and Apple featuring submissions.

Gameplay systems

I designed and implemented procedural cities, companion AI, combat, scavenging, squad progression and local two-player co-op. The game connects city expeditions with a home camp, where players prepare before heading out again.

Content editor

I built a browser-based editor for pixel art, sound and level assets. It exports Swift source directly into the game project, connecting content authoring to the SpriteKit implementation.

Launch & promotion

I contacted 60+ gaming video creators about promotion and manage the official X, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit and Discord accounts. I produce the trailer and store artwork, maintain the product and privacy websites, and prepare the game’s launch materials.

Release status

Pre-orders are open, with a worldwide App Store launch scheduled for December 3, 2026. The TestFlight beta, product website and trailer are linked above.
