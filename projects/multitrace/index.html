Investigation workflow

Built for a Deloitte competition, MultiTrace presents evidence collection, specialist analysis and review as a navigable workflow. The interface shows each role’s responsibilities and the handoffs between stages.

Evidence & human review

The demo connects sample evidence to an evidence graph and human review points. Visitors can inspect the proposed investigation flow and the information supporting each stage.

Demo scope

The linked demo is an interactive frontend simulation using sample data. It makes the proposed seven-role workflow tangible, with evidence graphs and human review points that a visitor can explore.
